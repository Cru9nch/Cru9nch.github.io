Privacy policy

NoteForge · Last updated 30 October 2026

NoteForge is a note-taking app. Its developer doesn't run any servers for it and doesn't collect, see, sell or share your notes or any information about you. There are no ads, analytics or tracking in the app.

Where your notes are kept

Your notes, PDFs, pictures and settings are stored on your device.

Your cloud account (if you sign in)

If you sign in to a cloud account in Settings › Cloud account, NoteForge copies your library to that account so it appears on your other devices, and keeps daily backups there:

  • Google Drive: NoteForge asks only for access to its own hidden app folder in your Drive (the drive.appdata permission). It can't see or change any of your other Drive files. It also reads your account's e-mail address to show who is signed in.
  • Dropbox and OneDrive: NoteForge works only inside its own app folder (under Apps) and reads your account's e-mail address to show who is signed in.
  • Nextcloud and WebDAV: NoteForge uses the folder "NoteForge Sync" on the server you choose.

The data goes directly between your device and your account; it never passes through anyone else's servers. Sign-in tokens and passwords are kept encrypted on your device. Signing out stops this; you can remove NoteForge's folder from your account at any time (in Google Drive: Settings › Manage apps).

NoteForge's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. That information is used only to keep your notes in your own account and is not used for anything else.

Features that use other services, only when you use them

  • Handwriting recognition: pictures of the pages you have recognised are sent to the provider you choose (Google Gemini or DeepSeek) with your own API key, under that provider's terms.
  • Chat: the chat panel opens the chatbot websites you choose, signed in with your own accounts there.
  • Backups: backups go only to the places you set up (this device, a folder, your network storage, your cloud account).
  • Maths: typesetting a formula loads the KaTeX library from the jsDelivr network.
  • Sharing: notebooks you share go to the app you choose in the share sheet.

Children

NoteForge isn't directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.
