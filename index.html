NoteForge

Handwritten notes for Android tablets, phones and foldables, made for the S Pen.

Write, read and study

  • Notebooks with paper templates, planners, infinite canvases and flashcards.
  • Pens, highlighters, shapes, layers, text boxes, tables and pictures.
  • PDFs, Word, PowerPoint and web pages to read and write on.
  • Handwriting recognition and search, links between notes, and a graph of them.

Your notes on every device

Sign in once with your own cloud account (Google Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive, Nextcloud or a WebDAV server) and NoteForge keeps your library there, in its own app folder, and on every device signed in to the same account. Your notes go only to your account; NoteForge has no servers of its own.

Privacy

NoteForge doesn't collect your notes or any information about you. Read the privacy policy.
